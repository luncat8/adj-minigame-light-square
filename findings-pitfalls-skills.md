Movement invariants must exercise the production move function: a mock that omits corner flips can pass reversal while proving nothing about the implementation.
A bounded search must check all depth-one moves before depth two; returning on the first depth-two win mislabels shorter solutions. In the seeded difficulty experiment, 2/8/20 scramble steps were not monotonic by sampled win rate (roughly 4.3–6.2% / 6.3–7.6% / 4.4–5.7%); do not equate scramble length with difficulty. Samples are 20-step random walks from the start, not uniform reachable poses.
Candidate scoring must be evaluated on independent walks: for 24 seeds per rule combination, targets 0.01/0.08/0.20 gave holdout means 0.0067–0.0135 / 0.0474–0.0667 / 0.1844–0.1895. These are distribution-level metrics, not individual difficulty guarantees; shallow wins and player experience still need testing.
