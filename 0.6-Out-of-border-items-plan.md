# 0.6 · Out-of-border items

Reserve a symmetric outer margin around the 15×15 board on the square canvas (`RIM_PAD` cells per side, mapping `[-RIM_PAD, 15 + RIM_PAD]` onto the canvas and offsetting board hit-testing by the same margin) and place small reactive items in that rim (`< 0` or `> 15` in board coordinates). They are pure visual feedback: activation derives from the 0.3 secret-contract evaluation and currently arriving border light (`traceRecv`), never from unlit `contract.requirements` (preserving hidden target locations when `Show targets` is off) and never feeding back into tracing or scoring. Render them in the same pass as the border receivers so one pass owns the whole rim.

Items:

- **Long tube with boiling water:** heater glow, rising bubbles and top-vent steam intensify from calm water to a rolling boil as quota progress rises to met.
- **Lamp:** filament and globe glow in the active RGB channel mix (`traceRecv` union) hitting border receivers, blooming with rays when met.
- **Detector:** pulses its scope and lights a multi-bar signal meter from `result.percent` (with a warning tint on forbidden extra light) as the contract moves from partial to met.
- **Magic crystal:** faceted shards shimmer partially on contract engagement and ignite with prismatic refraction when met.
- **Neon Chinese billboard:** displays RGB channel indicators and dynamic neon text (`待机` idle, `调光` / `过载` partial, glowing `成功` when met).
- **Power cell (bottom):** five charge segments fill with `result.percent` (warning tint on forbidden extra light) and glow when met; its terminal bridges the emitter rail.

Wiring: every lit border receiver is wired outward to its side's item(s). A stub from the receiver slot edge to a gap bus carries the arriving mask color; the bus (side-union color) runs along the rim and drops to the item center. The top side splits by column (lamp columns 1–3, billboard 4–11, crystal 12–13); the bottom bus feeds the power cell across the emitter rail. Wires glow additively when met. Only lit receivers are wired, never unlit `contract.requirements`.

Rules:

- Item states mirror the active 0.3 evaluation: **idle** (`M = 0` and no wrong-color target hit), **partial** (not met, with `M > 0` or a wrong-color target hit, including `M ≥ K` blocked by `E > 0`), and **met** (`M ≥ K` and `E = 0`).
- Explicit controls (CW/CCW buttons, keys `1`–`4`, emitter buttons, 0.5) update item state and the polite live readout identically to pointer input, so feedback is not vision-only.
- While previewing the witness (0.5), evaluate the witness trace purely without mutating live progress so items show the previewed contract state; ending preview restores the live state with the rest of the live view.
- Toggling hint overlays (0.3) must not alter item state; items track the contract evaluation itself, not the overlays.

**Acceptance:** reach idle, partial and met through normal play and confirm each item transitions with the board percentage; trigger one item per mapped state via explicit controls with all hints off; confirm lit receivers wire to their side item, unlit receivers stay unwired, and the bottom power cell fills with percent and glows when met; preview and release restores the previous item states; scoring and discovery history stay unchanged.
