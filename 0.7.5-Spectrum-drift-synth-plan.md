# 0.7.5 · Sound: Spectrum Drift synth variant

A sixth synth variant behind the Sound panel selector. The photon is a sustained broadband **noise** voice whose RGB mask acts as a three-band spectrum: R keeps the low band, G the mid band, B the high band, white keeps all three. Mirrors are purely subtractive — a split never adds energy, it only redirects the reflected bands while the passing bands continue untouched. Pan is driven by **travel direction** instead of cell column, with an aim setting that computes reflect targets relative to the band's current pan or absolute from the direction itself.

## Voice model (patchesSpectrum.js, new)

- One sustained voice per branch lineage: one looped noise source feeding three band filters (R lowpass 220 Hz, G bandpass 800 Hz Q 0.9, B highpass 2400 Hz), each band into its own gain and stereo panner, all into one voice output. White = three bands summing back to broadband hiss.
- Bands are independent streams: each carries its own active flag, travel dir, pan and tilted center frequency. A band belongs to exactly one live photon because `traceVis` partitions channels across branches, so per-band state never collides.
- The registry maps branch id → voice and is preallocated per wave (128 slots, 6 live voices max). A fork does **not** spawn a voice: the split event aliases the fork branch to the parent voice, so a split redirects bands inside the shared noise source and never doubles energy. Unknown branches (registry was full) spawn on their next event from that event's channels.
- A voice whose bands have all landed fades out, stops its source and frees a slot. `wave.hold` stops any still-alive voices at wave end.

## Event → band update

Notes carry the reflect channel bits (`ref`) next to `ch` so a split knows which bands turn. Per band in `ch`:

- **pass bits of a split** — nothing: the bands keep dir and pan, their next glass event drifts them.
- **reflect bits** (also the whole `ch` of `EV_REFLECT`) — dir := `dirOut`; pan target: absolute mode → direction table (left −0.9, right +0.9, up/down 0); relative mode → `pan − turn · 2.2 · drift`, so a left turn walks the band further left from where it was.
- **straight travel** (`EV_GLASS`, `EV_FILTER`) — drift one step toward the current direction's extreme per event: left −drift, right +drift, up/down × (1 − drift) toward center. Chained steps make an unobstructed beam reach the full side after a few cells, and the passing half of a split drifts home to center — the example behaviour.
- **land** (`EV_ABSORB`) — the band fades out and a soft sine ping at the band's (tilted) center sounds from the band's pan, scaled by note velocity; when no bands remain the voice dies and frees its slot.

Pan and filter ramps cancel-and-anchor per event (previous target as anchor), so chained glides never jump and overlapping ramps stay deterministic.

## Vertical cue

`tilt` shifts a band's center frequency by ±120·tilt cents from its travel dir: moving up (away) darkens, moving down (toward the player) brightens. Up and down share the center pan, so they need a timbral cue to be distinct.

## Params

| key | kind | range | default | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `aim` | choice | absolute / relative | absolute | reflect pan target rule |
| `drift` | range | 0.05–0.6 | 0.24 | pan step per straight event toward the direction extreme |
| `glide` | range | 0.04–0.6 s | 0.28 | pan/filter ramp time per event |
| `tilt` | range | 0–1 | 0.55 | doppler tilt of band centers for vertical travel |
| `reverb` | range | 0–1 | 0.5 | reverb send (read by the engine wet mix) |

Choice params are a schema extension: a param with `choices` makes the Sound panel build a `<select>` and the audio resolver accept the string value; numeric params behave exactly as before.

## Wiring

- `sonify.js`: note template and build loop carry `ref` (reflect channel bits from the event log).
- `patchesSpectrum.js` (new): owns the variant; header + files-map entry; `psNote` called from the registry.
- `patches.js`: registry entry with the param table and lazy call into `patchesSpectrum.js`; shared `paBed`.
- `audio.js`: `auResolvedParams` accepts choice params.
- `soundpanel.js`: storage validation and param-block rebuild handle choice params.
- `index.html`: script tag after `patchesFlow.js`, before `patches.js`; variant option in `snd-variant`.
