# 0.7.6 · Sound: Sound on checkbox and physical mechanism SFX

Replaces the `Sound off` toggle button with a `Sound on` checkbox (checked by default, persisted in `localStorage`, toggled by `M`), unlocks Web Audio on check and on any click while checked so Chromium's autoplay policy never leaves a checked box silent after the player's first gesture, and adds tactile physical sounds for concentric square rotations (low mechanism) and laser emitter movement along the bottom rail (`x` position).

## Sound on checkbox and gesture unlock (`index.html`, `soundpanel.js`, `audio.js`, `input.js`)

- `index.html`: `<label id="snd-on-wrap"><input type="checkbox" id="snd-on" checked> Sound on</label>` replaces `<button id="snd-on">`.
- `spCfg.on` defaults to `true` and persists in `localStorage` under `lightSquareSound`. Boot syncs `snd-on.checked` without constructing an `AudioContext` before a user gesture.
- **On check / uncheck:** `#snd-on` `change` updates `spCfg.on`, saves storage, and calls `soundSetOn(spCfg.on)` — checking immediately creates/resumes audio; unchecking halts waves/bed and suspends the context.
- **Unlock on any click when checked:** a capture-phase `document` listener (`click` and non-touch `pointerdown`, skipping `#snd-on-wrap` so unchecking via the label never triggers a transient unlock) plus input move gestures call `soundPanelUnlock()`. When `#snd-on` is checked, `soundSetOn(true)` creates the `AudioContext` on first gesture and calls `resume()` whenever the context is still `'suspended'`. When unchecked, clicks never enable sound.
- Master gain follows `auOn && !hidden`; `snd-mode` (`on move` / `loop` / `bed only` / `off`) governs photon waves and harmonic bed only, so physical mechanism sounds remain active whenever `Sound on` is checked.

## Physical mechanism synthesis (`soundfx.js`, new)

Routed through a dry `sfxBus` directly into the master limiter (bypassing the long reverb convolver so low-end transients stay close, tight and free of sub-tail mud) and tracked by `auRegisterVoice`.

- **Texture buffer:** one 0.6 s precomputed DC-blocked brown-friction + sparse micro-grain `AudioBuffer` per `AudioContext`, started from a stepped offset per gesture so consecutive turns have natural surface variation with zero per-move buffer allocation.
- **Square rotation (`fxRotate`):** low mechanism sound scaled by concentric ring mass (`7×7` outer 54 Hz, `5×5` middle 67 Hz, `3×3` inner 83 Hz, `1×1` center 102 Hz):
  - Low `triangle` chassis body (low-passed at 240–345 Hz so odd harmonics translate on small speakers while preserving sub weight on headphones) paired with an inharmonic `1.87×` rigid-frame `sine` mode and CW/CCW pitch-and-pan torque inflection.
  - Band-passed dark bearing friction swelling mid-turn.
  - Quarter-turn mode (`mode === 0`, rings 0–2) adds a subtle mid-arc ratchet pawl before a pitch-dropping final detent lock; single-slot mode (`mode === 1`) and center flip (`ring === 3`) play a tight single-tooth step and lock. Rapid drag bursts damp velocity to prevent buildup.
- **Laser rail movement (`fxLaser`):** horizontal carriage slide along X (`fromCol -> toCol`, columns `1..13`):
  - Stereo pan glides from `(fromCol - 7) / 6 · width` to `(toCol - 7) / 6 · width`.
  - Band-passed linear rail friction (`340..660 Hz`) and low-mid sled body (`128..192 Hz`) sweep continuously with normalized X position `(col - 1) / 12`, ending in a crisp column-index detent click at `toCol`. Pressing step left/right against rail stops (`1` or `13`) plays a muted end-stop thud panned to that stop.

## Ownership and wiring

- `soundfx.js` (new): owns physical mechanism and emitter rail synthesis (`fx*` prefix). Loaded before `audio.js`.
- `audio.js`: adds `sfxBus`, `soundRotate`, `soundEmitter`, suspended-context resume on repeat `soundSetOn(true)` and zero-sample wave cancel on same-tick unlock+move.
- `soundpanel.js`: owns `spCfg.on`, checkbox sync, `soundPanelUnlock` and document click/pointerdown gesture hook.
- `game.js`: calls `soundRotate` from `gameMoveRing` and `soundEmitter` from `gameEmitterStep` / `gameSetEmitter` (never during preview, generation or restart).
- `input.js`: calls `soundPanelUnlock` on canvas pointerdown, control buttons and move shortcuts.

## Validation

- Automated (`sound_smoke`, `dom_smoke`, `contract_fixtures`): 21-script classic boot with `soundContext() === null` and `snd-on.checked === true`; click anywhere unlocks when checked and stays silent when unchecked; checking/unchecking `#snd-on` and pressing `M` sync checkbox, storage and `AudioContext` state; ring rotations and emitter moves schedule `sfxBus` audio immediately with X-pan tracking column position.
- Manual pass list (Chromium, Firefox, Safari via `file://`):
  - Fresh load shows `Sound on` checked; first click anywhere (canvas, button, background) enables audio with no double-breath glitch.
  - Unchecking `Sound on` mutes immediately, survives reload, and clicking controls keeps audio off until checked again.
  - Outer -> middle -> inner -> center rotations step progressively from deep heavy mechanism to compact center clunk; CW and CCW have distinct torque feel; fast ring drags ratchet smoothly without clipping.
  - Moving the laser left and right glides smoothly across stereo X and rail pitch, with a soft bumper stop at columns 1 and 13.
