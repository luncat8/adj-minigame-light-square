# 0.4 · Solvable generation and difficulty

Use the move model (0.1), tracer (0.2) and win predicate (0.3) unchanged. Freeze movement mode and corner behavior per puzzle. A committed rule selection generates a new puzzle at once; restart keeps the active rules. A witness reachable by single-slot shifts need not be reachable by quarter turns.

1. Sample glass and a witness pose (all rings, center diagonal and emitter column). Trace it and derive the contract from actual hits. For strict tasks use the entire lit set and exact masks. For easy tasks choose a quota supported by the hits, accept any color and allow outside hits. To offer more choices, enlarge the pool with receivers seen in other reachable poses; do not assume every combination is reachable or that pool size alone makes play easier. Reject empty contracts.
2. Scramble from the witness with legal moves under the active rules. Reject starts already satisfying the task. Retain the witness and inverses of the scramble; replay from the start and require a win before offering the puzzle. The witness need not be unique or shortest.
3. Bound sampling and generation attempts. If the requested difficulty band is unavailable, prefer the closest validated candidate; if none qualify, use a replay-verified preset for the active rules. Never label an unchecked random board solvable.

With one white source, each channel follows one path; splitting routes colors but does not duplicate them. At most three distinct receivers can be lit together. A larger *pool* is fine; never request a simultaneous quota beyond the witness hits.

The slider targets *average* difficulty, not a guaranteed move count. Scramble length is not a difficulty measure: another emitter position may win immediately. In Node experiments sample seeds per movement mode, corner setting and slider band. Measure the fraction of sampled reachable poses that win, shortest depth on a bounded search subset (unreached is unknown), target count, rejection rate and generation latency. Choose bands with decreasing sampled win rates, then playtest whether extra search feels harder or just more tedious.

**Acceptance:** replay witnesses across seeded batches in both movement modes with corners on/off; zero invalid witnesses or already-won starts, including forced fallback. Report measured ranges before assigning slider labels. Manually compare several low/high pairs without peeking.
