# 0.7.9 · Noise1 default movement sound

## selector

`#snd-move` offers `noise1` (default), `natural` relabeled `noise2`, `drum` (drum
mechanism) and `bearing` (`s2`). Values `natural`, `drum` and `bearing` keep their
storage identity — saved choices and the s2/noise2 labels keep working unchanged; only
the default and the displayed name of the old natural foley change. Voice auto-switch:
Spectrum Drift → drum, every other voice → noise1. Manual choices stick until the next
voice change; missing or invalid stored `moveSound` falls back to the auto value.

## noise1 — felt glide

`soundfxNatural.js` gains `fxFlowMove` beside `fxNaturalMove` (noise2). Both share one
movement shell: velocity from move spacing, ring-mass timing and pitch tables, stereo
pan rail and the cached noise buffer. Per move noise1 layers:

- a broad band-pass glide swept with travel direction (CW rises, CCW falls; the rail
  sweeps across the crossed columns' resonance instead),
- a darker half-frequency mass layer,
- three short irregular air contacts at random offsets (never periodic),
- one soft broad settle near the end instead of slot knocks.

No pitched oscillator, no periodic LFO, no per-slot contacts: the step-like noise2
character stays available as the alternative, while noise1 reads as one continuous
tactile glide. Ring mass lowers and lengthens outer rings; quarter turns run long,
single steps and the center flip run short; velocity stacking and width panning behave
like the other mechanisms; every source stops inside one registered voice lifetime.
