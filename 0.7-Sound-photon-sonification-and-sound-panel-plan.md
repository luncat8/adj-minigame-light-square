# 0.7 · Sound: photon sonification and sound panel

Each pose sends an audible **photon wave** out of the emitter. It flies along the traced beam at a slow, adjustable speed. Every interaction it meets on the way plays a sound that tells what happened there: passing clear glass, passing a colored mirror, reflecting left or right, splitting into colors, landing on a receiver, or falling back to the bottom. A sustained **bed** layer holds the chord of the receivers that are lit now, and its tension follows the contract state. Several synth **variants** voice the same events. The player picks one in a Sound panel and tunes it there. Sound is pure output: it reads the trace and the contract result and never feeds back into tracing, scoring or discovery history.

## Event stream (trace.js)

The tracer already finds every interaction. It also records each one, so sound (and an optional visual photon) never has to re-derive propagation rules.

- **Depth:** each queue entry carries its arc length in cells from the emitter. The emitter device (row 14) is depth 0, and the first interior cell (row 13) is depth 1. Depth and branch pack above the 13 used bits of the queue entry (cell 0–7, dir 8–9, ch 10–12, depth 13–22, branch 23–30 — one positive Int32 entry). Border arrivals also step into the FIFO queue at `depth + 1` and log `EV_ABSORB` on dequeue without propagating further, so every event comes out in nondecreasing depth. Fixtures assert this, and consumers rely on it with no sorting.
- **Bend:** keep a per-(cell, dir) `Int8Array` with the signed sum of turns along the branch (left +1, right −1) and an `Uint8Array` with the bounce count, carried from the parent entry through `propagate` into `traceSeed`; the emitter seed starts at 0, and when two parents reach the same (cell, dir) the first one wins. They feed the contour pitch source and the vibrato depth.
- **Branch:** a saturating byte per entry identifies the ray a photon is on: the seed starts at 0, children inherit the parent's branch, and a split keeps the parent's branch on the continuing straight ray while the diverging reflected ray takes the next counter value. Events log `traceEvBranch` plus `traceEvFork` (the spawned child branch on `EV_SPLIT`, 0 otherwise) so per-branch variants link the diverged ray voice from its split origin.
- **Event log:** preallocated parallel typed arrays (`traceEvCell`, `traceEvDepth`, `traceEvBranch`, `traceEvFork` as `Uint8Array`, `traceEvInfo` as `Uint16Array`) plus `traceEvN`, with one write per event. Add getters next to `traceRecv` / `traceSeg`, and read them lazily at call time. `info` packs kind, incoming dir, passed and reflected channels and the outgoing reflect dir into 13 bits.

| kind | where | condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `EV_GLASS` | glass cell, clear tile | all channels pass |
| `EV_FILTER` | mirror tile | incoming channels ∩ mask = ∅ (pass through colored mirror) |
| `EV_REFLECT` | mirror tile | incoming ⊆ mask; turn = left/right relative to travel |
| `EV_SPLIT` | mirror tile | both parts non-empty |
| `EV_ABSORB` | first border cell | receiver arrival; bottom row = return |

Empty non-glass cells are silent and produce no event. Channels already visited at a (cell, dir) are deduped by `traceVis`, so they produce no event either: one sound per real interaction. The cost is a few stores per processed entry. Measure it with the generation fixture timing (`gen_ms` before and after) and accept it if |Δ| ≤ 5%.

## Score (sonify.js, DOM-free)

`sonifyBuild(view)` snapshots the event log into its own preallocated **score** at publish time. Trace buffers are reused by the next trace (preview, generation), so the snapshot happens before anything else can retrace. Each note has these fields: `depth` (cells from the emitter), `cell` (board index), `branch`, `fork`, `kind`, `midi` (float), `pan`, `panTo`, `vel`, `ch`, `bright`, `turn`, `dirOut`, `bounce`, `status`. The bed descriptor has the chord (MIDI list), `tension` 0..1, `beat` (Hz detune for forbidden light) and `state` (idle/partial/met).

Mappings, shared by every variant:

- **Time:** derived from `depth` at schedule time, never baked into the score — a speed change then applies live and never needs a re-sonify. Speed is in cells/s (1–16, default 4, so a straight 14-cell shot takes 3.5 s). Notes with equal depth form a chord. The score keeps event order — depth is nondecreasing and every mapping below is monotone in depth — so it is consumed without sorting; musical settings (scale, root, pitch source) bake into `midi` and apply to the next wave.
- **Space:** pan comes from the cell column (−1..1) × width. `panTo` is the pan half a cell along the outgoing direction, so each note *glides* toward where the light goes and horizontal travel is audible as motion. Row gives `bright`: near the top is brighter and drier, near the emitter darker and wetter.
- **Direction:** travel up gives a small upward pitch glide (+15–30 cents over the note). Travel down, toward the player, glides down and sounds darker. A left turn and a right turn use different intervals or timbres per variant (by default left resolves up a scale step and right falls a step). Return to the bottom row is a falling, filtered tone.
- **Color:** a channel picks a chord tone and a timbre slot: R = root, G = fifth, B = third/ninth, and white = triad. Velocity scales with √(channel count), so a split keeps its total energy and three single-channel beams are each quieter than white.
- **Pitch source (setting):** `position`: the cell maps to a scale degree (column → degree, row → octave band), so the same cell always sounds the same and the board is learnable. `contour`: start degree + bend × 2 + bounce, so the melody traces the path's shape.
- **Scale and root (settings):** major pentatonic (default), minor pentatonic, lydian, whole tone, hirajoshi. Pitches stay inside the scale, so a busy board still sounds consonant.
- **Receivers:** rim index k (52 cells, clockwise from the top-left) maps to a degree, so each receiver has its own note. Side sets pan, and arrival color sets the chord.
- **Leak rule:** per-receiver match/wrong/outside timbre is used **only** when `view.overlays.current` is non-null (the Current matches hint is visible and not previewing). Otherwise a receiver sounds by position and color alone, and fixtures assert that the score is identical whether that cell is a target or not. The bed uses only readout-visible facts: `contractState`, `percent`, `wrongColor`, `forbiddenLight`.
- **Bed:** the chord is the notes of the lit receivers. Tension maps idle → a sparse root drone, partial → added tones with tremolo LFO rising 0.2 → 2 Hz with percent, and met → resolution to a full consonant chord with a slow 0.1 Hz breathing LFO and shimmer. Forbidden extra light adds a beating detuned pair. A wrong color adds a soft minor-second rub.
- **Caps:** at most 6 notes per depth and 160 per wave. Overflow merges equal-depth notes of the same kind (union of channels), then drops by priority: absorb > split > reflect > filter > glass. `experiments/score_dump.js` measures event counts over generated puzzles to confirm the caps (`events_med`, `events_max`, `dur_med_s`, `chord_max`).

## Engine (audio.js)

- **Lifecycle:** the `AudioContext` is created lazily on the first user gesture that enables sound (autoplay policy). Suspend it on `visibilitychange` hidden and while muted. Resume on return.
- **Graph:** wave bus + bed bus → gentle tanh `WaveShaper` → dry, plus a send to a `ConvolverNode` → `DynamicsCompressor` (limiter role) → master gain → destination. The reverb impulse response is generated in JS: stereo decorrelated noise × exponential decay, darkened by a one-pole filter. Its length is a setting. It is regenerated debounced, so there are no audio files and it stays file:// friendly.
- **Scheduler:** lookahead pattern. A `setInterval` (25 ms) schedules score notes falling within `currentTime + 0.12 s` through a cursor over the depth-ordered score. No per-frame allocation outside note creation. Web Audio source nodes are one-shot by design, so they are created per note and released on `ended`.
- **Waves:** each wave owns a `GainNode` and the score snapshot it started with, so a later retint (hint republish, settings change) only shapes the next wave. A pose change is debounced by 120 ms so drag bursts make one wave. The old wave bus then ramps to 0 over 80 ms (no clicks), stops and disconnects, and the new wave launches with a soft emitter "breath" (noise swell, rising band-pass); a loop relaunch timer dies with the wave, which is also all generate and restart need. The bed crossfades over 400 ms.
- **Play modes (setting):** `on move` (one wave per pose change, the default), `loop` (relaunch after the wave ends plus a gap, or quantized to a bar in the Pulse variant), `bed only` (no photons) and `off`.
- **Preview:** the witness score plays through a ghost insert (low-pass 1.4 kHz, +reverb, −6 dB) and the bed switches to the previewed result. Release cancels the wave and restores the live bed. A **win**, meaning a transition of live progress to `won` and never in preview, plays one resolution flourish: an upward arpeggio through the lit receivers' notes.
- **Polyphony:** at most 32 live voices. The oldest voice is released with a 30 ms ramp.

## Variants (patches.js)

Each variant is one table entry: `{ id, label, params: [{ key, label, min, max, step, def }], note(ac, bus, n, p, when), bed(ac, bus, b, p, when) }`. The panel builds its controls from `params`, so adding a variant needs no panel code.

1. **Glass Harp** (default). A reflect plays a 2-op FM bell (ratio 3.5 for a left turn, 1.4 for a right turn, index decaying with the envelope). A filter pass plays a soft sine "hum" with a slow attack and vibrato depth growing with bounce. Clear glass plays a 15 ms band-passed noise tick. Absorb plays a long bell with a chord. Long reverb. Params: bell decay, brightness, tick level, reverb.
2. **Prism Pad**: continuous and closest to a flying particle. One sustained voice per branch: two detuned saws hard-panned ±, with a ±4-cent phase drift for width, into a resonant low-pass. At each event the voice *glides* (portamento) to the new pitch, its pan ramps toward `panTo`, and the filter envelope opens on a reflect. A split spawns a voice that diverges in pitch and pan (at most 6 branches). At a receiver the voice keeps sustaining and hands over to the bed. Params: glide, cutoff, resonance, detune.
3. **Comet** (spatial, best on headphones). Per branch, a noise + sine "whoosh" runs through an HRTF `PannerNode` that follows the photon position in the board plane (listener at the emitter, facing up). Pitch shifts like a Doppler effect from radial velocity (approaching is higher). A reflect plays a short ping into a ping-pong delay synced to speed. Params: doppler amount, delay feedback, air (noise mix).
4. **Pulse**: rhythmic and always looping. The photon's steps quantize to 16ths at tempo = speed. Clear glass plays a closed hat. Left and right reflects play low and high toms pitched by position. A split plays a clap. A filter pass plays a rim shot. A receiver plays a plucked bass note plus chord stab. The board becomes a groove, and solving completes the pattern. Params: swing, drum tone, bass level.
5. **Crystal Grains**: ambient. Each event emits a cloud of 8–24 sine grains (20–60 ms Hann envelopes, scattered ±40 ms) on chord tones by color. Density follows channel count, and the scatter widens with bounce. The grains run into a damped feedback delay. Params: density, grain size, scatter, feedback.

All five ship behind the selector. After the listening pass the keepers are recorded in findings, and pruning is a separate cleanup.

## Sound panel (soundpanel.js + index.html)

A `Sound` fieldset in `#side` (ids prefixed `snd-`, checked against window-id collisions):

- `snd-on` toggle button (`aria-pressed`). Its first press creates and unlocks audio.
- `snd-variant` select, `snd-volume` slider, `snd-speed` slider (cells/s), `snd-mode` select (on move / loop / bed only / off).
- `snd-replay` button sends a fresh wave for the current pose.
- `<details id="snd-more">` holds the shared settings (scale, root, pitch source, stereo width, reverb, bed level) and the selected variant's params, generated from its table. Switching variants rebuilds only this block and keeps per-variant values.
- Settings persist in `localStorage` under one key, and a missing or invalid stored value falls back to the defaults. Key `M` (in input.js `onKey`) toggles mute. Labels and tooltips describe what each control does.

## Ownership and load order

- `trace.js`: adds the event log, depth and bend buffers (feature).
- `sonify.js` (new): owns the event → score mapping, scales, pitch sources and the leak rule. Edit for mappings and caps.
- `patchesFlow.js` (new): owns continuous branch-following and granular variants (`Prism Pad`, `Comet`, `Crystal Grains`). Edit for portamento, HRTF Doppler and grain clouds.
- `patches.js` (new): owns the synth variant table, param schemas, struck/groove variants (`Glass Harp`, `Pulse`), harmonic bed, emitter breath and win flourish. Edit for registry and struck/bed timbres.
- `audio.js` (new): owns the AudioContext, master bus, reverb impulse response, scheduler, waves, modes and the `soundView` sink. Edit for timing, mixing and lifecycle.
- `soundpanel.js` (new): owns the Sound panel DOM, settings state and persistence. Edit for controls and defaults.
- `game.js`: `publishView` also calls `soundView(v)` as a sink next to `renderView`. The view gains a `moved` flag — true from refresh (moves, generate, restart) and preview start, false from the hint republish and preview end — because `publishView` serves both and buffer identity never changes in place, so sound cannot infer a pose change on its own. `soundView` reads panel settings at call time, builds the score via sonify, and derives the live/preview/won transitions from the view it saw before.
- `app.js`: calls `soundPanelInit()`. `index.html`: fieldset markup and load order `… skins.js, sonify.js, patchesFlow.js, patches.js, audio.js, soundpanel.js, game.js, input.js, app.js`.
- The node-shim const prefixes stay unique: `so*` sonify, `pf*` patchesFlow, `pa*` patches, `au*` audio, `sp*` soundpanel. Update the AGENTS.md file map when the files land.

**Optional, separate step:** a visible photon. render.js draws a bright head on the segments whose depth equals the current wave front. The front comes from the audio wave clock, or from its own clock when sound is off, so the eyes and ears follow the same particle.

## Build order and validation

1. trace event log + fixtures (L1: trace + generate fixtures, timing log).
2. sonify + `experiments/sonify_fixtures.js` + `score_dump.js` (L1).
3. audio engine + Glass Harp + minimal panel (on/variant/volume/speed) + game sink (L2: shared files touched).
4. Remaining variants + full panel + persistence (L1 dom_smoke).
5. Listening pass and tuning of defaults (manual list below).

**Fixtures:**
- **Trace:** an empty column gives one absorb at depth 14. A full mirror gives a reflect with the correct turn sign for both diagonals. A partial mask gives a split with both channel sets. Depth is nondecreasing. A split keeps the parent branch on the continuing ray and gives the diverging ray a fresh one. Bend accumulates along the branch and stays deterministic on merge (first parent wins), and return-to-bottom is an absorb.
- **Sonify:** pitches stay in the scale and pan stays in range. Caps hold. The score preserves event order (nondecreasing depth, no sort). Under the leak rule a target and a non-target receiver score identically when hints are off. The preview flag sets ghost, and bed tension is monotonic in percent.
- **sound_smoke + dom_smoke:** the `AudioContext` stub records calls. No context exists before the gesture. A pose change schedules nondecreasing start times. A drag burst gives one wave. A hint republish launches no wave; generate and restart end the running one. Preview hold and release cancels and restores. Mute and hidden suspend. Settings round-trip through the localStorage stub. The vm scope check covers 19 scripts.

**Manual pass list:** on headphones and on speakers, in Chrome, Firefox and Safari via file://, check for each variant:
- A straight shot is audible as one flight.
- Left and right reflects are distinguishable eyes-closed.
- A split is heard as two diverging voices.
- The receiver side is clear from pan.
- Return to bottom sounds like falling.
- A fast drag gives no click pile-up or clipping.
- Loop mode stays pleasant for 2 minutes.
- Mute is instant.
- Preview sounds ghosted.
- The win flourish plays once.
- Settings survive a reload.
- Audio CPU stays low in the browser performance panel.
