### sound generator and settings panel to it

make some interesting and good sounding synth, that react on current laser configuration.

example:

imagine light particle pass from laser emoter and slowly fly - every time it hit obstacle it sound depends on what happens: is sound pass, reflected, in what direction.

you can use stereo, phase, tone, volume, low-freq envelope, other good sounding techniques to produce responsive, interesting soundscape.

because this difficult to predict what will work - better make some variants to choose.
