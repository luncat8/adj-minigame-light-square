# 0.3 · Secret contract, progress and discovery

A task records a pool of eligible border cells, a positive quota K ≤ pool size, a color requirement per eligible cell (any nonzero light or an exact RGB mask), and whether outside hits are forbidden. Tell the player the quota and rules, not the locations by default. Count distinct receivers in the **current pose**, never the union of discoveries across turns.

- Easy: meet **at least** K, in any combination from the pool, with any nonzero color. “Light 2 of 5” accepts any two together, or more; there is no designated pair and light elsewhere is harmless. For the same board, lowering K or adding eligible choices cannot invalidate a winning pose.
- Strict: require every listed cell with its exact color and no outside hits. A superset of the requested color is not an exact match.

Let M be the number of matching receivers and E the number of lit outside receivers if forbidden (zero otherwise). Display floor(100 × min(M,K) / (K+E)). Win exactly when M ≥ K and E = 0. For an unmet quota, distinguish a wrong color from missing light; do not report an unlit optional receiver as missing when the quota is met. Report forbidden extra light separately.

Three independent hint checkboxes, initially off:

- **Current matches:** solid outline and check for a matching receiver; dashed outline and half-mark for a lit eligible receiver of the wrong color (right cell, wrong light). Unlit or outside cells get neither.
- **Remember discoveries:** retain the best status each receiver reached in actual play (match outranks partial). Show a small historical badge distinct from the live outline, even after the light moves away. Track with the checkbox off too; toggling changes visibility only. Preserve discoveries on restart of this puzzle; clear them on a new puzzle.
- **Show targets:** mark every eligible cell, even unlit ones, with its required color or an any-color symbol. This reveals choices, not a prescribed subset or solution pose.

**Acceptance:** test K-of-N below, at and above quota, permitted and forbidden extras, exact color versus a superset, and matches on separate turns. Manually combine the three overlays: current, past and target must remain distinguishable by shape, not hue alone. Hiding hints or viewing a solution (0.5) must not change score or discovery history.
