# 0.3 · Secret contract, progress and discovery

A task records a pool of eligible border cells, a required count, a color requirement per eligible cell (any nonzero light or an exact RGB mask), and whether extra lit cells are forbidden. The pool and requirements are secret until revealed. On easy tasks, any required number from the pool suffices; ignore light elsewhere. On strict tasks the required count equals the pool size and no outside receiver may be lit. Evaluate the current pose only, not the union of discoveries across turns.

Proposed progress: let M be the number of lit eligible cells satisfying their color requirement, K the positive required count, and E the number of lit outside cells if extras are forbidden (otherwise zero). Display floor(100 × min(M,K) / (K+E)). Win exactly when M ≥ K and E = 0. This keeps 100% meaningful even when every requested color is right but a stray ray remains. Give short reasons: missing light, wrong color, or extra light; do not disclose locations by default.

Optional live outlines distinguish a match from a lit eligible receiver with the wrong color. A separate discovery toggle marks previously matched or partially matched cells with a distinct historical symbol; history never affects scoring. Keep history per puzzle, clear it on a new puzzle. The target hint reveals the pool; proposed behavior is to reveal required colors too. Solution preview is separate from both hints (0.5).

**Acceptance:** test K-of-N with extras allowed, exact color versus a superset, all correct plus one forbidden hit, and matches found on separate turns. Manually distinguish current, past and revealed targets without relying on hue alone.
