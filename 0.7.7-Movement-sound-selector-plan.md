# 0.7.7 · Movement sound selector and rusty bearings

The drum-like movement sounds from 0.7.6 suit only the `Spectrum Drift` synth voice. Every other voice gets a completely different, more prolonged movement sound: a rolling rusty-bearing wash with tonal accents marking each slot change. A new GUI selector chooses the movement sound; switching the synth voice auto-selects it (`Spectrum Drift` -> drum mechanism, every other voice -> rusty bearings), and a manual choice sticks until the voice changes again.

## Movement sound selector (`index.html`, `soundpanel.js`)

- `index.html`: `<select id="snd-move">` directly after `#snd-variant` in the Sound fieldset, with options `drum` ("drum mechanism", the 0.7.6 sound, unchanged) and `bearing` ("rusty bearings", the new prolonged sound). Title states the auto rule and that a manual choice sticks until the voice changes.
- `spCfg.moveSound` (`'drum' | 'bearing'`) persists in `localStorage` under `lightSquareSound` alongside the existing keys. Default `bearing` (default voice `glass` is a non-Spectrum voice).
- **Auto-select on voice change:** `#snd-variant` `change` sets `moveSound` to `drum` when the new voice is `spectrum`, otherwise to `bearing`, syncs `#snd-move`, saves storage and notifies audio. A manual `#snd-move` change sets `moveSound` directly and persists; it is not overwritten until the next voice change.
- **Boot / legacy storage:** when stored `moveSound` is present and valid it is kept (so a manual override survives reload); when absent (pre-0.7.7 storage) or invalid it is derived from the stored voice by the same auto rule. `spSyncControls` syncs both selects from `spCfg`.

## Movement sound routing (`audio.js`)

- `auDefaultCfg` gains `moveSound: 'bearing'`.
- `soundRotate` / `soundEmitter` read `width` and `moveSound` from the live settings at call time and pass both to `soundfx.js`, so a selector or auto-switch takes effect on the very next move with no graph rebuild. `game.js` and `input.js` are unchanged (they already call `soundRotate` / `soundEmitter` per move).
- Both sounds stay on the dry `sfxBus` into the master limiter, gated only by `Sound on` (never by `snd-mode`), exactly as in 0.7.6.

## Drum mechanism (`soundfx.js`, unchanged)

`fxRotate` / `fxLaser` with `moveSound === 'drum'` (and any non-`bearing` value) run the 0.7.6 synthesis verbatim: short low chassis thump + inharmonic frame mode + pawl + detent lock for squares, rail slide + sled body + column detent for the laser. The end-stop thud for `fromCol === toCol` is shared by both sounds (a bumper, not a drum).

## Rusty bearings (`soundfx.js`, new)

Prolonged rolls (roughly 4x the drum durations) built from the same precomputed brown-friction buffer, the same ring-mass base pitches (`54 / 67 / 83 / 102 Hz`) and the same CW/CCW torque pan, so the two sounds differ in character and length, not in loudness or direction:

- **Square rotation (`fxRotateBearing`):** durations `0.58 / 0.52 / 0.46 / 0.36 s` (quarter) and `0.38 / 0.36 / 0.34 / 0.34 s` (single / center) for rings outer -> center. Layers, all through one `out` gain registered once with `auRegisterVoice`:
  - Rolling rumble: `triangle` at the ring base pitch with a slow attack and a long release.
  - Rusty friction: looped brown buffer through a broad band-pass at the ring rumble frequency (`135 / 165 / 205 / 250 Hz`), amplitude-fluttered by a `6-9 Hz` LFO (balls passing), plus a faint high scrape (`~1100 Hz` band-pass) and 2-3 sparse grain clicks (short `~1800 Hz` noise bursts at seeded offsets) for rust flakes.
  - Tonal slot accents: one metallic ping per slot that changes — `side - 1` accents (`6 / 4 / 2`) for quarter turns on rings 0-2, one accent for single-slot moves and the center flip. Accents are spaced evenly across the roll (`16%..84%` of the duration) with pitch stepping in whole tones from `4x` the ring base (`216 / 268 / 332 / 408 Hz`): ascending for CW, descending for CCW, so direction is audible. Each ping is a sine with a slight pitch drop plus a faint `2.76x` inharmonic partial; the final (landing) accent is louder and drops an octave as the detent lock.
  - Rapid repeats damp gain (same `dt` velocity curve as the drum) and shorten the roll by 25% so drag bursts do not pile up.
- **Laser rail (`fxLaserBearing`):** duration `0.30 + 0.045 * steps` (`steps = |toCol - fromCol|`, capped at 6). The rail band-pass (`340..660 Hz` by normalized X) and sled body (`128..192 Hz`) sweep from `fromCol` to `toCol` with the same flutter treatment, pan glides `(col - 7) / 6 * width`, and one tonal accent per column stepped through (pitch `300 + 28 * (col - 1) Hz`, final column louder with the detent drop). The shared end-stop thud covers `fromCol === toCol`.

## Ownership and wiring

- `soundfx.js`: owns both movement sounds (`fx*` prefix); `fxRotate` / `fxLaser` dispatch on `moveSound` (`'bearing'` -> bearing, otherwise drum). Loaded before `audio.js`, unchanged.
- `audio.js`: owns `moveSound` in `auDefaultCfg` and passes it through `soundRotate` / `soundEmitter`.
- `soundpanel.js`: owns `spCfg.moveSound`, the auto rule (`spectrum -> drum`, else `bearing`), `#snd-move` sync/binding and the storage schema.
- `index.html`: owns the `#snd-move` markup and load order (unchanged).

## Validation

- Automated (`sound_smoke`, `dom_smoke`): boot with legacy storage (no `moveSound`) derives `bearing` for `glass` and `drum` for `spectrum`; voice change auto-switches `#snd-move` and `spCfg.moveSound` both ways; manual `#snd-move` choice persists across panel reinit and survives reload but is re-autoed on the next voice change; corrupt `moveSound` falls back to the auto value. Drum moves keep the 0.7.6 short durations and single detent; bearing moves schedule prolonged rumble/friction (stop times `> 0.3 s` out), one accent per slot with ascending (CW) / descending (CCW) pitches, and per-column laser accents with X-tracking pan. Classic 21-script boot stays clean with no `AudioContext` before a gesture.
- Manual pass list (Chromium, Firefox, Safari via `file://`):
  - Fresh load (voice `Glass Harp`) shows `rusty bearings` selected; switching to `Spectrum Drift` flips the selector to `drum mechanism`, switching back flips to `rusty bearings`; manually picking the other sound sticks until the voice changes again, and survives reload.
  - `Spectrum Drift` + drum: outer -> center rotations and laser slides sound exactly as in 0.7.6.
  - Other voices + bearings: rotations roll for roughly half a second with audible ball flutter and a tonal ping per slot (6 pings on an outer quarter turn, 1 on a single step), CW rising and CCW falling; the laser rolls and pings per column; fast drags stay controlled without clipping.
  - Balance: bearing rolls sit at a comparable loudness to the drum hits against each wet photon voice on speakers and headphones.
