# 0.6 · Out-of-border items

Place small interactive items in the margin just outside the 15×15 border (coordinates < 0 or > 14). They are pure visual feedback: activation derives from the 0.3 secret-contract result (receiver hits, quota K, color mask) and never feeds back into tracing or scoring. Render them in the same layer as the border receivers so one pass owns the whole rim.

Examples:

- **Long tube with boiling water:** bubbles and steam intensify when the quota or an RGB mask is met.
- **Lamp:** glows or changes color with the active channel (R, G, B or white) hitting a receiver.
- **Detector:** pulses or shows a signal strength bar as the contract moves from partial to met.
- **Magic crystal:** shards light or changes refraction when the beam terminates at the designated border receiver.
- **Neon Chinese billboard:** displays dynamic text or symbols (e.g. "成功", channel indicators) when the target is reached.

Rules:

- Item states mirror the live 0.3 result: idle, partial (M < K), met (M ≥ K and E = 0).
- The explicit controls (CW/CCW buttons, keyboard, 0.5) update item state exactly like pointer input, so the feedback is not vision-only.
- While previewing the witness (0.5) items show the previewed contract state; ending the preview restores the live state with the rest of the live view.
- Hiding hint overlays (0.3) must not alter item state; items track the contract itself, not the overlays.

**Acceptance:** reach idle, partial and met through normal play and confirm each item transitions with the board percentage; trigger one item per mapped state without hints shown; preview and release restores the previous item states; scoring and discovery history stay unchanged.
