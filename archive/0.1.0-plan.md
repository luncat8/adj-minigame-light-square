# 0.1.0 base engine

## board

15x15 grid. Border cells (row or column 0 and 14) are targets. The centered 7x7 is glass. Four concentric rings: 7x7, 5x5, 3x3, 1x1. Ring slots are ordered clockwise; a quarter turn maps top-left to top-right and flips mirror diagonals. Rotation permutes cell objects, it does not rebuild the grid.

## cells

Cell kind is empty, glass, or border. Cell type is pass or mirror. A mirror stores its diagonal (0 is `/`, 1 is `\`) and a reflect mask (R=1, G=2, B=4). Pass cells transmit all channels.

## optics

A beam starts below the board at `laserStart` and travels north. Each step moves one cell, records a segment, and ORs the beam mask into `cell.light`. A mirror splits the mask into reflected (`mask & cell.mask`) and passed (`mask & ~cell.mask`) channels. Reflection is `dir ^ 1` for `/` and `3 - dir` for `\`. Beams are processed from a preallocated stack with step and segment caps.

## generation

Build random glass, choose solved ring turns, pick a laser column inside the glass with the most lit border cells, trace that state, and take up to N lit border cells as targets. Scramble the rings by 1-3 quarter turns per ring. The inverse scramble is a valid solution. Reject a scramble that is already solved.

## render

The canvas fills the window. The board is centered with a bottom margin for the laser. Draw grid, border and glass tiles, mirror diagonals, additive beam strokes batched by mask, target diamonds, laser emitter, and status text. Geometry is recomputed on resize.

## input

Pointer move sets the laser column. Wheel over a ring rotates it. Drag around the board center; a 0.35 rad turn rotates the ring under the pointer. A/D or arrows move the laser, 1-3 set width, N generates a new puzzle.

## validation

`node --check js/*.js`, `node experiments/engine_smoke.js`, and `node experiments/dom_smoke.js`. The engine smoke covers quarter-turn identity, mirror reflection, channel splitting, and solvability of 20 seeded puzzles. The DOM smoke boots all scripts against a mocked canvas and draws one frame.
