Implemented the `Sound on` checkbox with Chromium gesture unlock and physical mechanism SFX for square rotations and laser rail movement:

### 1. `Sound on` Checkbox & Chromium Click Unlock (`index.html`, `soundpanel.js`, `audio.js`, `input.js`)
- Replaced `<button id="snd-on">` with `<label id="snd-on-wrap"><input type="checkbox" id="snd-on" checked> Sound on</label>` (checked by default, persisted in `localStorage`, toggled with `M`).
- **On check / uncheck:** `#snd-on` `change` immediately enables/resumes or halts/suspends Web Audio.
- **Any click while checked:** capture-phase `document` listeners (`click` and non-touch `pointerdown`, skipping `#snd-on-wrap` so unchecking via the label never triggers a transient unlock) plus move gestures call `soundPanelUnlock()`, creating the `AudioContext` on the first gesture and calling `resume()` whenever Chromium still holds the context `'suspended'`. No `AudioContext` is created before a user gesture or while unchecked.

### 2. Square Rotation & Laser X-Movement SFX (`soundfx.js`, `audio.js`, `game.js`)
- Added `soundfx.js` routed through a dry `sfxBus` into the master limiter (bypassing the long convolver reverb so low-frequency transients stay tight and mud-free at any reverb setting):
  - **Squares rotation (`fxRotate`):** low mechanism sound scaled by concentric ring mass (`7×7` outer `54 Hz`, `5×5` middle `67 Hz`, `3×3` inner `83 Hz`, `1×1` center `102 Hz`), combining a low-passed `triangle` chassis body, an inharmonic `1.87×` rigid-frame `sine` mode, DC-blocked brown bearing friction, CW/CCW pitch-and-pan torque inflection, an intermediate pawl tick on quarter turns, and a pitch-dropping detent lock.
  - **Laser movement (`fxLaser`):** horizontal carriage slide along X (`columns 1..13`), gliding stereo pan from `fromCol` to `toCol` alongside X-mapped rail bandpass friction (`340..660 Hz`), a low-mid sled body (`128..192 Hz`), a precision column-index detent click, and a soft bumper stop when stepping against columns `1` or `13`.
