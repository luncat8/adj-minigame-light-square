- focused headless check: white ray into a red mirror produces kind=3 split with
  refCh=1 (red, low) deflecting RIGHT and passCh=6 (G|B) straight; the reflected red branch
  carries ch=1 with dirOut=RIGHT on every downstream event, so the accumulator drifts toward
  +1 (100% stereo) when unobstructed. At the split, 2 stereopanners are created: deflected
  voice target 0.12 (drifts), passthrough voice target 0 (centered) — matches the brief.

decisions and why:
- model color = spectrum (R low / G mid / B high); white = full noise. this is the literal
  "subtractive from noise" reading and makes a colored mirror audibly narrower than white.
- a colored mirror hit by white is a *split* in this engine, not a pure reflect, so the brief's
  "low freq moves left, other freq move to center" is realized by spawning two voices at the
  split: reflected color (deflected, drifts) + passthrough (straight, centered). refCh/passCh
  were required to separate the two.
- `drift` one-slider blend of relative (accumulating source) vs absolute (board column) covers
  both readings of "relative to previous direction | absolute" without a non-slider control.

pitfalls:

- tiles array passed to traceLight is GLASS*GLASS (49), not GLASS_OFF*GLASS_OFF (16); an
  undersized buffer silently drops out-of-range writes and the mirror never engages. full note
  in findings if revisited.
