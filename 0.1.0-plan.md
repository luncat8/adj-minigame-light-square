# 0.1 · Board and player moves

The board is 15×15, including a one-cell outer border. With zero-based coordinates, the glass occupies rows and columns 4–10. The intervening cells are empty and transmit light. A puzzle stores its initial arrangement separately from the player's moves; restart restores that arrangement and the initial laser position.

One input moves only the selected ring, clockwise or counterclockwise. For a ring of side S > 1, enumerate its 4(S−1) perimeter slots clockwise, counting each corner once. The puzzle's movement mode sets the stride:

| Ring | Slots | Quarter-turn mode: slots per move | Single-slot mode: moves per lap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 7×7 | 24 | 6 | 24 |
| 5×5 | 16 | 4 | 16 |
| 3×3 | 8 | 2 | 8 |

Four quarter-turn moves make one lap on every non-singleton ring. A quarter turn uses the same slot transitions as S−1 single steps; it is not a second rotation of each tile. RGB masks travel with their tile. With the corner-turn checkbox off, keep the diagonal fixed on the board. With it on, mark the four clockwise edges *leaving* corner slots; traversing a marked edge in either direction flips the diagonal. Thus a quarter turn crosses one marked edge per tile, and reversing a move undoes it exactly.

**1×1 exception:** either direction flips the center mirror in place (`/` ↔ `\`) in both modes, regardless of the corner setting. Two flips restore it. A clear center remains clear.

The bottom emitter slides one interior column at a time (columns 1–13), firing one thin white beam upward. **Deferred, low priority:** adjustable width up to three adjacent columns. Wider settings would emit light in every covered column, not just thicken the drawn line. Width selection and multi-column generation/testing are outside the current scope.

**Acceptance:** on distinctly labeled rings, verify the strides and lap lengths above, compare a quarter turn with its single-step equivalent, and check move/inverse restores every tile, diagonal and mask in both corner settings. Other rings must not change. Manually check corner reversal, center flip and both emitter stops.
