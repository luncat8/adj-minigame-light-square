# 0.1 · Board and player moves

The board is 15×15, including a one-cell outer border. With zero-based coordinates, the glass occupies rows and columns 4–10. The intervening cells are empty and transmit light. A puzzle stores its initial arrangement separately from the player's moves; restart restores that arrangement and the initial laser position.

A move shifts the selected ring's tiles by one perimeter slot, not by a quarter turn of the whole square. The 7×7, 5×5 and 3×3 rings have 24, 16 and 8 slots respectively. Both directions are legal. The corner-turn setting controls whether a mirror changes diagonal when its tile turns a corner. For a reversible convention, mark the four clockwise edges *leaving* corner slots; crossing a marked edge in either direction toggles the diagonal. RGB masks travel with their tile.

Proposed singleton rule: a step on the 1×1 ring turns its tile in place by 90°, independent of the corner setting. A clear tile remains clear. Proposed emitter rule: slide horizontally by whole columns, across the interior width, firing upward. Widths 1, 2 and 3 occupy adjacent columns; position refers to the leftmost column and stops before any beam would run along a side border.

**Acceptance:** each move followed by its inverse restores every tile, orientation and mask in both corner modes. One perimeter lap restores the ring. Moving one ring leaves the others unchanged. Manually check reversal at a corner, the center tile and both emitter stops at each width.
