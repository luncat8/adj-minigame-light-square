# 0.7.5 · Spectrum Drift synth — draft

User prompt (2026-11-22):

> make new sonification synth based on next idea:
>
> color is spectrum. color filter - subtractive from noise. reflection move sound in direction then sound source move to it relative to previous direction | absolute according to settings. Example white ray move up sound noise, it hit red mirror and low freq will start to move to left (pan, and if no obstacles after some time reach 100% left stereo) and other freq start to move to center (pan).
>
> think good how to improve. implement

Reading notes:

- "color is spectrum" — a ray's RGB mask selects which frequency bands of a noise source stay audible: R = low band, G = mid band, B = high band, white = all three. Hearing the mask replaces hearing a pitch.
- "subtractive from noise" — a dichroic mirror never adds energy: the reflected beam keeps only its own bands of the very same noise, the passing beam keeps the rest. Subtraction is structural, not an effect.
- "reflection move sound in direction" — pan is driven by travel direction, not cell column: a turn glides the band's pan toward the new direction and straight travel keeps drifting toward that direction's extreme, so an unobstructed beam ends pinned at 100% left/right; up and down both pull the pan back to center.
- "relative | absolute according to settings" — a variant setting picks how a reflect computes its pan target: relative accumulates the turn delta on top of the band's current pan, absolute maps the outgoing direction to a fixed pan position.
- "think good how to improve" — vertical travel needs its own cue because up/down share the center pan (doppler tilt of band centers); a receiver landing should say which color landed (per-band ping); a voice whose bands all landed should free its slot; a split must not duplicate noise energy (forks alias the parent voice).
