# 0.2 · Light transport

Input is a board pose and emitter column (0.1). Output is a color mask per visible beam segment and per border receiver. The renderer and goal checker consume the same trace. Emit white (R|G|B) upward just inside the bottom border in that column; emission is not a receiver hit. The first border cell reached absorbs the arriving light, including light returning to the bottom.

Clear glass and empty space transmit all channels straight. A diagonal mirror has a reflect mask: reflected channels turn 90° according to `/` or `\`, and the remaining channels continue straight. A full mask is an ordinary mirror. Example: white incident upward on `/` with R|B reflect gives magenta to the right and green upward. Crossing beams do not steer one another. Combine arrivals at a receiver by channel union, not last-hit color or intensity.

Trace with a work queue and a visited mask per (cell, incoming direction). Propagate only previously unseen channel bits for that entry. A repeat direction with a *new* color must still propagate. This bounds work even for a closed path. Reuse trace buffers and recompute on pose/rule changes, not on each paint.

**Acceptance:** Node fixtures cover empty board, both diagonals from all sides, split, combined receiver hits, return to bottom and a closed-loop trace seeded inside the loop. Results are independent of queue order. Visually follow a split ray through the glass and the empty space to both endpoints.
