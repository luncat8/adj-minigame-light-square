# 0.5 · Play surface and controls

Draw a centered square board on black, with legible translucent glass, diagonal faces, short animated RGB beam dashes flowing from the emitter to hit receivers (visually distinct from solid mirror diagonals), and clearly separated border receivers. Preserve the beam centerline even with glow. Show the selected ring before moving it.

- Wheel over a ring (or the emitter row) to step it exactly once per physical wheel detent, independent of the OS lines-per-notch setting, delta mode or a hi-res wheel splitting one detent into several events: the first event after a 120 ms pause (or a direction flip) steps at once; inside a burst a step fires when the accumulated delta crosses the middle of the next detent of the learned size; one event never steps twice. Detent size is learned from short isolated bursts (≤ 200 ms) as the lower quartile of the last 8 burst totals, so multi-detent flicks and stray nudges do not skew it. Hover selects the ring. A drag along its perimeter accumulates signed distance; emit one move per active stride (one side for a quarter turn, one slot for a single shift). Lock the ring for the gesture. Clicking the center flips it. Drag the emitter or use left/right controls.
- Show the active movement mode and corner rule. Offer mode, corner-turn checkbox and preferred difficulty slider beside Generate; changing a selection alone does not replace the puzzle. The slider has three bands implemented in generate.js: 0 few targets (quota ⌈h/2⌉ of the witness hits, any color, outside allowed), 1 all witness hits (any color, outside allowed), 2 strict (every hit, exact colors, outside forbidden). Restart restores its start, not its witness.
- Keep percentage and mismatch feedback beside the board. Use separate checkboxes for Current matches, Remember discoveries and Show targets (0.3).
- **Preview solution:** hover the button with a hover-capable pointer, or hold it by press (touch, mouse, Space or Enter). Temporarily draw the saved witness: move the displayed laser **and** all rings, including the center, to that pose and trace its light. Label it as a preview, not a player win. Render from the witness rather than playing moves into the live board. Suspend play input, leave player progress and discoveries untouched, and suppress live hint overlays while previewing.
- End preview on release (even if still hovering), pointer leave/cancel, focus loss or window blur. Require a fresh entry or press to start again. Restore the exact live view, not the start pose. Genuine completion leaves the winning board visible until the player restarts or generates a puzzle.

**Integration:** open index.html via file://, using ordered classic local scripts, no build or network dependency. Separate board moves, tracing, goals, generation, drawing and controls by owner; the composer only wires them together. Keep rule logic DOM-free for Node fixtures. `experiments/dom_smoke.js` boots the page under a stub DOM and canvas in Node and drives the real handlers: wheel, drag, emitter drag, preview hold/leave/blur/keys, restart, generate and win replay.

**Acceptance:** manually generate in each mode, slide the laser, move each ring both ways, inspect split colors and combine hints. From a noninitial pose, preview by mouse, touch and keyboard; check release and interrupted gestures restore the same pose, trace, progress and badges. Win normally and generate again. Confirm scrolling outside the board still works and resizing retains square cells. Run the shared integration smoke after wiring.
